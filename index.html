Words appear. You type them. The clock runs out and you get one number. No account, no settings page, nothing attached.

This is the iPhone app, live. Try it.

The test, and only the test

One number

Words per minute, accuracy, raw speed, characters. Then you are done.

Five modes

15, 30 or 60 seconds. 25 or 50 words. Or a quote. Punctuation and numbers optional.

Keyboard only

Tab restarts. Wrong letters go red, backspace fixes them. No mouse needed.

Nothing leaves your device

Your best score per mode stays in the browser. No account, no tracking, no server.
